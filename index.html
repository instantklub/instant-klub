Who We Are

More Than Just Noodles

INSTANT KLUB is Nepal's first dedicated Korean ramyeon cafe, born from a simple idea: that a humble bowl of instant noodles can become an experience worth craving.

We bring the world's best instant noodles — from the legendary Buldak lineup to Shin Ramyun, Neoguri, Chapagetti and beyond — and let you customize them with a full bar of toppings, snacks, and Asian drinks.

What started as one outlet has grown into a movement. Today we have 6 outlets open across Nepal with 2 more in the pipeline, and we're just getting started.

🥢 Shin Ramyun

Shin Ramyun (Original)

The global standard for spicy Korean ramyeon. A sharp, peppery beef broth accented with shiitake mushrooms, garlic, and scallions.

🌶️ Medium-Hot

Shin Black

A premium upgrade with a second Sul-Long-Tang (bovine bone broth) sachet. Thicker, creamier, and more garlic-forward, softening the sharp spice.

🌶️ Medium

Shin Red (Super Spicy)

An intense, stripped-down formula geared purely toward high heat tolerance. Twice as hot as the original.

🌶️🌶️🌶️ Extreme

Shin Gold

A milder, comforting spin-off that infuses a creamy, rich chicken broth base into the classic pepper profile.

🌶️ Mild

Shin Kimchi

Features an added pouch of freeze-dried pickled kimchi flakes, introducing a tangy, sour punch to the beef broth.

🌶️ Medium

Shin Stir Fry

A dry, sauce-based version delivering an intense, sweet-spicy concentrated paste.

🌶️🌶️ Hot

Shin Toomba

Mimics the viral Korean creamy-spicy rose pasta sauce trend.

🌶️ Mild-Medium
